**Task:** Given a raw, messy, unedited discovery-call transcript involving **multiple stakeholders**, produce a finished requirements document.
**Deliverable structure:**
1. Every statement tagged by altitude, source, and standing
2. Any genuine stakeholder conflicts identified and routed to the correct escalation contact — not smoothed into a vague compromise
3. A finished requirements document per [Module 10](/altitude/module-10)'s structure, with status flags on every topic
4. A vertical-traceability pass ([Module 6](/altitude/module-06)) on at least one topic: does the confirmed L2 actually achieve the L0 it's attached to? If not, the observation and how it would be raised with the client
5. A short section addressing how at least one topic in the transcript would need to be re-checked if a related statement surfaced later during delivery (applying [Module 12](/altitude/module-12))
